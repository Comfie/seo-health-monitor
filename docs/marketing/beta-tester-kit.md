## Positioning

**One-liner:** SEO Health Monitor audits your website like a search engine would, shows exactly what is holding it back, with proof, and tells you what to fix first.

**Who it's for:**

| Who | Why they care |
| --- | --- |
| Small-business owners | They want more enquiries from Google and don't know what's wrong with their site. |
| Freelance web designers and developers | They want a quick, credible audit to show clients and a clear fix list to work through. |
| Small agencies and marketers | They need repeatable audits and before-and-after progress for clients. |

**What makes it different (use 2 or 3 per post):**

- **Proof, not guesses.** Every issue shows the exact page and what was found. If something can't be measured, it says "not verified" instead of making it up.
- **Prioritised.** Each issue is labelled *Fix first*, *Fix next* or *Plan later*, based on impact and effort.
- **Real Google data.** Speed tests come from Google PageSpeed Insights. Clicks, impressions and index status come from your own Search Console, read-only.
- **Hands you the fix.** One click copies step-by-step instructions you can paste into AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Cursor or Copilot or send to your developer.
- **Shows progress.** Compare scans to see what was fixed, what's new and how your score moved.
- **Local and conversion checks.** It checks phone numbers, click-to-call, WhatsApp links, contact forms, location pages and LocalBusiness data, not just technical SEO.

**The beta offer:**

- free during the beta, by invitation;
- 2 websites, 5 full audits a month and up to 25 pages per audit;
- PDF, CSV and Markdown reports.

**What we ask:** run at least one audit, then spend 10 minutes on feedback.

## Feedback questions

Use these in Google Forms or Typeform, or ask them in a short call.

1. What do you do? (business owner / web designer or developer / marketer or agency / other)
2. Which platform is your site built on?
3. How easy was it to sign up and run your first audit? (1–5)
4. Did the results match what you know about your site? Was anything wrong or surprising?
5. Which three findings were most useful?
6. Was anything confusing or hard to act on?
7. Did you use **Fix with AI** or a report? How did it go?
8. Did you connect Search Console? If not, why not?
9. How likely are you to keep using it after the beta? (0–10)
10. What would you pay for it monthly, if anything? (nothing / under R100 / R100–R300 / R300–R700 / more)
11. What's the one thing it should do that it doesn't?
12. May I quote you, by first name and business? (yes / no)

## Answers for comments and DMs

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is it really free? | Yes, during the beta. Pricing isn't set yet, and testers will hear first. |
| Will it break or slow my site? | No. It only reads public pages, a few at a time, and respects robots.txt. |
| Do you need my passwords or admin access? | No. Just the website address. Google Search Console is optional and read-only. |
| Does it work on Wix, WordPress, Shopify…? | Yes. It audits the public site whatever it's built on, and recognises the platform so the fix instructions fit it. |
| Is it AI making things up? | No. Findings come from fixed checks on real data. AI is only used, optionally, to help you implement the fixes. |
| How long does an audit take? | Usually a few minutes, depending on the site's speed and size. |
| Where's my data stored? | In an isolated account on servers in the EU. You can export or delete your data yourself at any time. |
| Can I audit a client's site? | Yes. Any public website. Search Console data needs the site owner's Google access. |

## Running the beta

- **Track invites:** name, email, website, date invited, date signed up, feedback received.
- **Pace invitations to the capacity:**
  - the system runs 2 audits at once and allows 400 PageSpeed tests a day, which is about 65 audits a day;
  - 20–30 testers in the first wave is comfortable.
- **Before posting widely,** check [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md#before-inviting-testers-widely). In particular:
  - add testers' Google accounts as test users if they want Search Console;
  - finalise the privacy page and its contact email.

## Claims to avoid

- Don't promise rankings, traffic or "page one". The product finds and prioritises problems; results depend on fixing them.
- Don't call it AI-powered analysis. The checks are deterministic, and AI only helps with implementation.
- Don't claim Google partnership or endorsement. It *uses* Google's public APIs.
- Don't claim it checks reviews, Google Business Profile, rankings or competitors. Those show as not verified.
